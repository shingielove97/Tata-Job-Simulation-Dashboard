# Tata Visualisation(Interactive Dashboard using Pbix)

## Project Objective
The CEO and CMO want the online retail store’s data analysed in order to identify the key drivers of revenue, evaluate performance from both operational and marketing perspectives, and provide data-driven insights that will support strategic planning, business expansion, and targeted decision-making based on customer demographics.
### Dataset used
<a href="https://github.com/shingielove97/Tata-Job-Simulation-Dashboard/blob/main/Online%20Retail%20Data%20Set.xlsx">Dataset</a>
## Business Questions

1. Analyze monthly revenue trends for 2011 to identify seasonality and support forecasting.
2. Identify top 10 revenue-generating countries (excluding UK), including quantity sold.
3. Determine top 10 customers by revenue, ranked highest to lowest, to support retention strategies.
4. Assess global demand by country (excluding UK) to identify high-potential regions for expansion.

 ## Process
1. Cleaned data by removing negative values (returns/errors).
2. Applied validation checks: quantity ≥ 1 and unit price ≥ $0.

## Key Insights
1. Revenue remains stable $685k for the first 8 months.
2. Significant growth begins in September +40%, peaking in November $1.5M
3. Indicates strong seasonality in the final quarter (December data incomplete).
4. High-performing countries (excluding UK): Netherlands, Ireland, Germany, France.
5. Revenue is well distributed across customers, no heavy reliance on a few buyers.
6. Sales are concentrated in Europe; minimal presence in the Americas, none in Africa/Asia.

## Conclusion

1. Align marketing with seasonal demand spikes.
2. Invest in high-performing countries to drive growth.
3. Strengthen retention strategies for top customers.
